Forensic Explanation: What Constitutes an ARP Anomaly?

DEFENSIVE PROTOCOL

1. The Baseline Binding

In standard IPv4 networks, an endpoint maps its logical IP address to a physical 48-bit MAC address via ARP requests and replies. NETGUARD records these bindings to create an empirical baseline.

2. The Mapping Change

An alert is raised when a packet arrives asserting that an established IP address is now located at a different MAC address. This causes neighboring hosts to overwrite their ARP tables.

3. Attack vs. Legitimate Reassignment

Crucial Rule: Mapping changes do not automatically prove malicious action. Legitimate causes include DHCP lease expiry, access point roaming, NIC replacement, and OS MAC randomization. Always correlate with 802.1X and switch MAC tables.
